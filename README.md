# 📚 NeuronIQ Library

Welcome to the **NeuronIQ Library**, a curated repository of content, books, and reference materials hosted for **NEURON-IQ**.

This repository serves as the central storage for reading materials, academic textbooks, and literature used by the NEURON-IQ project.

🔗 **Main Project Repository**: [NEURON-IQ](https://github.com/Kavyargb/Neuron-IQ)

---

## 📖 Library Contents

Here is an overview of the books and resources currently hosted in this library:

### 🎓 Academic & Textbook Resources

*   **Calculus** (by James Stewart)
    *   *Description*: Comprehensive study of calculus, organized into chapter-wise PDFs (Chapters 1 to 17).
    *   *Path*: [`Calculus (James Stewart)/`](./Calculus%20(James%20Stewart)/)
*   **Introduction to Linear Algebra** (5th Edition, by Gilbert Strang)
    *   *Description*: The standard textbook for linear algebra concepts, organized into chapter-wise PDFs (Chapters 1 to 13).
    *   *Path*: [`Introduction to Linear Algebra Ed 5 Gilbert Strang/`](./Introduction%20to%20Linear%20Algebra%20Ed%205%20Gilbert%20Strang/)
*   **Mathematics for Computer Science** (by Eric Lehman, F. Thomson Leighton, and Albert R. Meyer)
    *   *Description*: Mathematical foundations for computer science, covering logic, proof, induction, and probability.
    *   *Path*: [`Mathematics for Computer Science (Eric Lehman, F. Thomson Leighton etc.).pdf`](./Mathematics%20for%20Computer%20Science%20%28Eric%20Lehman%2C%20F.%20Thomson%20Leighton%20etc.%29.pdf)

### 🎭 Literature, Strategy & Humanities

*   **The Art of Seduction** (by Robert Greene)
    *   *Description*: An analysis of social power, persuasion, and character profiles (e.g., Siren, Rake, Coquette).
    *   *Path*: [`The Art of Seduction/`](./The%20Art%20of%20Seduction/)
*   **The 48 Laws of Power** (by Robert Greene)
    *   *Description*: A classic handbook on the dynamics of power and influence.
    *   *Path*: [`The 48 Laws of Power.pdf`](./The%2048%20Laws%20of%20Power.pdf)
*   **Dangerous Liaisons** (by Pierre Choderlos de Laclos)
    *   *Description*: The classic epistolary novel exploring intrigue, seduction, and power.
    *   *Path*: [`Dangerous Liaisons.pdf`](./Dangerous%20Liaisons.pdf)
*   **Poems by John Keats**
    *   *Description*: A collection of classic Romantic poetry by John Keats.
    *   *Path*: [`Poems by John Keats.pdf`](./Poems%20by%20John%20Keats.pdf)
